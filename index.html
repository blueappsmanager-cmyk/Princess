1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Bunny Blast - Cute Block Puzzle, published by BlueAppsBox ("we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to ensuring a secure and family-friendly experience for all players.

2. Information Collection and Use

Bunny Blast - Cute Block Puzzle does NOT collect, store, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

  • No Account Registration: All 300 levels can be enjoyed without signing in, registering emails, or sharing credentials.
  • No Device Media or Contact Access: The app never requests access to your photos, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • No Precise Location Tracking: We do not track or store your GPS location.

3. Local Storage Data (On-Device Only)

The App uses HTML5 LocalStorage strictly on your local device to save:

  • High Scores & Unlocked Levels: To remember your achievements across Levels 1 to 300.
  • Audio Preferences: To store your Sound On / Mute settings.

This data stays strictly on your personal phone and is never uploaded to external servers.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob SDK

The Game is supported by non-intrusive advertisements served through the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Optional rewarded video ads allow players to revive when no moves remain.

Google AdMob may collect technical pseudonymous identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) — resettable anytime in your Android device settings.
  • Basic device model, OS version, and network country.
  • Ad interaction and rewarded ad completion events.

5. Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Family & Child Safety: Bunny Blast - Cute Block Puzzle is safe for children and families. We adhere strictly to COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy guidelines. All ad requests follow child-directed settings where required.

6. Data Deletion & Control

You have full control over your local game data:

  • Clear Storage: Go to Android Settings > Apps > Bunny Blast - Cute Block Puzzle > Storage > Clear Data to wipe all local stats.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all stored game state.
