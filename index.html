1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which BlueAppsBox ("we", "our", or "us") operates the arcade mobile game Princess Run - Pac Prince Maze (Package: com.blueapps.pacmanprincess).

We are firmly committed to maintaining user trust by ensuring that your personal privacy is unconditionally protected. Our game operates with zero personal data collection: you can enjoy all 300 palace levels without creating accounts, providing credentials, or sacrificing privacy.

2. Zero Personal Information Collected

Strict No-Collection Policy: BlueAppsBox does NOT collect, harvest, store, sell, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to remote servers.

When you download, install, or run Princess Run - Pac Prince Maze, we never request, access, or store:

  • Personal Details: Your real name, physical address, age, national ID, or phone number.
  • Contact Information: Your personal email address, device phonebook, or social media accounts.
  • Geographic Location: Precise GPS coordinates or real-time location tracking.
  • Device Sensors & Media: Microphone recordings, camera feeds, photos, videos, or external SD card files.
  • Financial Details: Banking information, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

3. Local Device Storage (Offline Game State)

To deliver a seamless offline arcade experience, the application utilizes standard HTML5 local storage (localStorage) stored strictly on your local device hardware. This data never leaves your device:

  • frogman_highscore: Stores your highest achieved score across all games.
  • frogman_max_level: Tracks your unlocked palace progression (Palace 1 up to 300).
  • frogman_muted: Preserves your audio sound effect preferences.

You can erase all saved progress at any time by selecting Clear Data / Clear Cache in your device's Android Application Settings or clearing browser storage.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob SDK

Princess Run - Pac Prince Maze is free-to-play and integrates Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob).

AdMob enables banner ads and optional Rewarded Video Advertisements (which allow players to annul a wedding proposal and gain +1 Life to continue running).

Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device telemetry in accordance with standard advertising protocols:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) used for fraud detection, ad capping, and relevant ad serving.
  • Diagnostic Telemetry: General network operator, device model, operating system version, and crash data.
  • Ad Engagement: Verification of completed rewarded video views to grant in-game continues.

User Control: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Application Permissions

Princess Run - Pac Prince Maze requests only standard, non-sensitive Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required strictly to load AdMob ad units and web assets.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to verify network availability.

No dangerous or sensitive runtime permissions are requested.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Princess Run - Pac Prince Maze is designed as wholesome, family-friendly casual entertainment suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or share personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

7. Data Security Architecture

Because Princess Run - Pac Prince Maze operates client-side and does not operate backend authentication servers or remote user databases, your game interactions are immune to remote database breaches.
